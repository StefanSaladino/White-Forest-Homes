Our building process

What to expect when you renovate, add on or build a custom home with White Forest.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    We talk through your goals, budget and timing, and look at the property when it helps define the scope.

  2. 02

    Design, planning & proposal

    We work with your design team as needed to refine the scope, review materials and costs, and prepare a proposal. We coordinate permits and scheduling before construction.

  3. 03

    Construction & project management

    We manage the work on site, coordinate trades and keep you informed as construction progresses.

  4. 04

    Final walkthrough

    We walk through the finished work with you, review the details and address any outstanding items.
