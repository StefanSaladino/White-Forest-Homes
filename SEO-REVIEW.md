Collingwood, Ontario is the primary market. Keep nearby service areas general until confirmed. The website has never launched; this is an initial search strategy, not a ranking migration or traffic forecast.

| Page | Search intent |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Collingwood home renovations, renovation contractor, custom home builder |
| Services | Kitchen and whole-home renovations first; home additions, custom home construction and finish carpentry as supporting services |

The October 31 Keyword Planner exports cover October 2025–September 2026. The kitchen renovations row shows 50 in the narrower export and 500 in the expanded export; home renovations, whole house renovation and custom home builder show 50 in both. Home additions shows 0 and 50, while home addition contractors shows 50 in both. These are Planner estimates for the selected targeting, not confirmed Collingwood-only search counts or Search Console results. No demand claim is made for custom carpentry. Renovations and kitchens lead because the terms fit the work and the customer’s priorities.

The page titles, visible copy and social metadata now support those intents. Canonicals, social URLs and the existing social image use the configured production domain. About and Contact page content, image captions, gallery behavior and the five-link navigation remain as designed. The sitemap includes the retained public pages. The thank-you page is crawlable so its existing noindex directive can be read.

Before launch: confirm the production host and domain, keep any hosted development preview private or noindexed, then verify canonical URLs, share image responses and the sitemap on production. Submit the production sitemap in Search Console. Once data accrues, review non-brand queries by landing page; only add dedicated landing pages if search or Ads needs justify them.
