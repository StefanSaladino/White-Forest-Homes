Questions

Planning your project.

When should I start planning a renovation?

Start with your goals, property details and an approximate timeline. Reviewing the existing home early helps identify the scope and the professionals needed before work begins.

Can an addition connect naturally to the existing home?

The layout, structure, rooflines and exterior finishes all need to be considered together. The right approach depends on the property and is developed during planning.

Can I stay in my home during the renovation?

That depends on the scope, access and which services or rooms will be affected. Phasing and any temporary relocation should be discussed before construction starts.
