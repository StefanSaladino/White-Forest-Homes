Questions

Planning your project.

What size renovations do you take on?

Project fit depends on scope, location, timing and current capacity. The inquiry form helps the team understand the work before scheduling a conversation.

Can you renovate an older cottage?

Existing buildings often require careful investigation before final scope and pricing. The team can discuss the appropriate review process for the property.

Can we remain in the home during construction?

That depends on the scope, utility interruptions, safety requirements and schedule. Occupancy expectations should be agreed upon before work begins.
