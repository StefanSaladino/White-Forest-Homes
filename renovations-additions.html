A practical first conversation.

We’ll discuss the property, intended scope and next steps. For an addition, flag any available plans and known property constraints early so design and permit requirements can be considered before construction.

Before you enquire

Can we discuss both a renovation and an addition?

Yes. Describe what you want the finished home to do; the initial conversation can cover changes within the existing footprint and potential extra space.

Do I need drawings first?

You can enquire while exploring ideas. If drawings already exist, mention them in your inquiry so the next conversation starts with the right information.

What should I include?

Your property location, rooms or spaces involved, target timing and any initial budget range.
