Common questions

Before we begin.

Where does White Forest Homes work?

The company is based in Huntsville and serves Muskoka and surrounding communities. Exact service boundaries should be confirmed for each project.

Does White Forest Homes take on both new builds and renovations?

Yes. The current service offering includes custom homes, renovations, additions, kitchens, decks and several related residential construction services.

How early should I contact the team?

As early as practical. An initial conversation can clarify timing, project readiness, design requirements and the information needed for next steps.
