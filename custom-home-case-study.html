The Project

A home with a consistent point of view.

White Forest built this custom home with a material palette that carries from the kitchen to the loft. White cabinetry and light walls give the interiors a bright backdrop. The timber island, vaulted ceiling and staircase bring texture and warmth.

Each space uses those materials differently. The kitchen pairs a broad work surface with tall cabinetry, while the stair nook and loft rooms offer quieter corners of the home.

Custom home · Room by room

Inside the custom home.

The kitchen

A timber-topped island anchors the white kitchen. The vaulted ceiling opens up the room, and tall cabinetry keeps storage close to the range and work surfaces.

The staircase and reading nook

The timber staircase leads to the loft, with a reading nook tucked beside it. The woodwork ties the passage between floors back to the kitchen.

The loft bedroom

Light walls and timber details give the loft bedroom a quieter take on the finishes used downstairs.

The loft washroom

The loft washroom carries the lighter palette into another part of the upper floor.
