Privacy policy.

How White Forest Homes handles website inquiries, browser storage, analytics and advertising measurement.

OverviewInformation collectedHow information is usedCookies & measurementService providersRetention & safeguardsYour choicesContact

Last updated: July 29, 2026. This policy describes the website configuration supplied with the redesign. It must be updated whenever the company’s actual data practices or vendors change.

1. Overview

White Forest Homes respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact the company. This policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, when it may be shared and what choices are available.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit the project inquiry form, the website may collect your name, email address, phone number, property location, project type, approximate investment range, target timing and the project details you choose to provide.

Technical and usage information

If you consent to optional measurement, Google Analytics and Google Ads may process information about website visits, interactions, device or browser characteristics, approximate location, referral information and advertising interactions. The exact information depends on the final Google account settings and consent status.

Necessary browser storage

The website stores your cookie preference in local browser storage under wfh_consent_v1. This preference is used to remember whether optional analytics and advertising categories are permitted.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate project fit, communicate about requested services, operate and secure the website, understand website performance when consent is granted, measure Google Ads conversions when advertising consent is granted, meet legal obligations and protect the company’s rights.

Information submitted for a project inquiry is not automatically consent to receive unrelated promotional email.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The supplied website defaults optional analytics and advertising consent to denied. Google tags are not downloaded unless a visitor grants at least one optional category through the cookie interface.

  • Necessary: Supports core website functions, security and storage of the visitor’s privacy choice.
  • Analytics: Allows Google Analytics to measure website use and content performance.
  • Advertising: Allows Google Ads conversion measurement and advertising-related storage.

You may accept, reject or change these choices at any time through the link in the footer.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by providers that support website hosting, form delivery, email, security, analytics or advertising. The supplied build is prepared for Netlify Forms and Google measurement tools, but the final vendor list must reflect the services actually enabled at launch.

Personal information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a legitimate business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention and safeguards

Personal information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and for applicable business, legal or record-keeping requirements. Reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards should be used based on the sensitivity of the information.

7. Access, correction and withdrawal of consent

You may ask about personal information held by White Forest Homes, request corrections or withdraw consent where applicable, subject to legal or operational limitations. Withdrawing optional cookie consent does not prevent you from using the site or submitting a project inquiry.

8. Processing outside Canada

Some service providers may process information outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of another jurisdiction. The final policy should identify material cross-border service arrangements used by the business.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in technology, vendors, legal requirements or business practices. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be directed to Whiteforesthomes@hotmail.com or 1-705-970-6325.
