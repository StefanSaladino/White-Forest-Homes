The person behind White Forest

Danny Jenkins

Mason & home builder · Collingwood, Ontario

Danny Jenkins' passion for building began at just 16 years old, when he started studying masonry in the United Kingdom. What began as an interest in traditional craftsmanship developed into a lifelong career in residential construction.

After spending years working alongside experienced builders throughout the UK, Danny moved to Canada at 24, bringing with him a strong foundation in masonry and hands-on construction. He continued developing his expertise working with several established builders, gaining experience across renovations, additions, and custom home construction.

In 2016, Danny took the next step in his career and established his own building business. Since then, he has dedicated himself to delivering exceptional craftsmanship, managing projects with a hands-on approach, and building lasting relationships with homeowners.

With over 25 years of experience, Danny combines traditional building knowledge with modern construction practices, bringing skill, precision, and a genuine passion for quality to every project.
