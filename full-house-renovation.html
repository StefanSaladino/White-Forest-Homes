The Project

One home. Three spaces up close.

White Forest completed a whole-home renovation that brought a consistent set of finishes into rooms with different needs. In the kitchen, warm cabinetry and task lighting surround a central island with room to work and gather.

The two washrooms take different approaches to layout. One brings a floating vanity and glass shower into a compact room; the other pairs a freestanding bath with a double vanity and a separate shower. Dark fixtures and pale surfaces connect them visually.

Explore the renovated spaces.

The kitchen

A dark island holds the sink and gives the kitchen a central work surface. Tall wood-toned cabinetry frames the cooking area, with under-cabinet lighting along the tiled backsplash.

Washroom 1

A dark floating vanity and a glass shower fit into a compact layout. Light floor tile and a slim mirror light keep the finishes simple.

Washroom 2

A freestanding bath sits beside the windows opposite a double vanity. The separate glass shower has dark fittings, large-format tile and a recessed niche.
