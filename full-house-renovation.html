Case study · Full-house renovation

A full-house renovation, room by room.

A separate White Forest renovation project, with a kitchen and two distinct washrooms. Wood tones, dark accents and lighter surfaces connect the spaces while giving each room its own character.

Discuss your renovationExplore all projects
The project

One house. A coordinated renovation.

White Forest completed a full renovation of this home. The supplied photographs focus on the kitchen and two separate washrooms, showing how the work comes together across different parts of the house.

The rooms share a restrained palette, but they serve different purposes. The kitchen centres on a large island; one washroom has a compact arrangement with a shower and floating vanity, while the second combines a freestanding bath, double vanity and separate glass shower.

Kitchen

Wood tones and a bold central island.

Wood-look cabinetry gives the kitchen a warm backdrop. The dark island contrasts with the lighter perimeter surfaces, while under-cabinet lighting picks out the tiled backsplash.

Kitchen details

Space for preparation, storage and gathering.

The island brings the sink, storage and an overhanging counter into the centre of the room. Tall cabinets and a run of drawers frame the cooking area, with a stainless-steel range and hood set against light tile.

A linear pendant defines the island from above. Recessed lights and lighting beneath the wall cabinets give the room several layers of illumination. The photographs show the same finishes from the island, range and side views.

Washroom one

A compact washroom with a floating vanity.

The first washroom has its own layout: a dark floating vanity, light floor tile and a glass shower with dark hardware. A framed mirror and slim light fitting keep the vanity area simple.

Washroom two

A freestanding bath and a separate shower.

The second washroom is a different room. A dark-sided freestanding bath sits by the windows, opposite a double vanity. A separate glass shower uses large-format wall tile, dark fittings and a recessed niche.

Across the house

Consistent finishes. Different room layouts.

The kitchen and washrooms use related contrasts: warm cabinetry or dark vanities against lighter walls, with dark fixtures and clear, simple lines. The two washrooms carry that approach into separate layouts rather than repeating the same arrangement.

This project adds a full-house renovation to the White Forest portfolio, alongside the church kitchen and custom home. Explore the photographs at full size to see the cabinetry, surfaces, lighting and washroom details.
